1. Welcome & how Lexy works

Lexy is an AI-powered hiring platform. You stay in control of every decision; Lexy's team of AI agents does the heavy lifting: reading job descriptions, finding and scoring candidates, drafting outreach, running structured screening interviews, verifying profiles, and writing client-ready evaluation reports.

The agent team behind the scenes

Agent What it does Where you see it
ICP Agent Reads your job description and extracts an Ideal Candidate Profile — skills, seniority, certifications, disqualifiers, location. Work Order → ICP tab
Sourcing Agent Searches internal talent first, then external providers, and ranks matches against the ICP. Work Order → Sourcing tab, Agent Hub
Screening Agent Screens resumes against the role, producing a recommendation and matched/missing skills. Candidate profile → Resume Screening
Outreach Agent Drafts personalized first-touch emails in your brand voice. Drafts wait for your approval by default. Outreach → Pending approvals
Conversation Agent Classifies inbound replies (interested, not now, unsubscribe) and drafts responses. Inbox
Interview Agent ("Lexy") Conducts live, voice-based structured interviews, grades every answer, and writes a summary. Interview room, interview reports
Verification Agent Cross-checks candidate claims (identity, employment consistency) and raises flags. Candidate profile → Verification
Anti-Ghost Agent Watches for silence and no-shows, and nudges at the right moments. Anti-Ghost, Work Order → Anti-Ghost tab
Intelligence Agent Continuously accrues a multi-dimension candidate score as new evidence arrives. Candidate profile → Intelligence
Digest Agent Compiles your Morning Report and daily priorities. Dashboard
Golden rule Lexy proposes, you decide. Outreach requires your approval before sending, stage moves you make manually always win over agent gates, and every AI evaluation is presented as evidence for your decision — never a verdict.

2. Getting started: login, roles & navigation

Logging in

  1. Open your Lexy workspace URL in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
  2. Sign in with your work email and password, or via your company's Microsoft single sign-on if enabled.
  3. If you don't have an account, ask your Recruiter Admin to invite you from Team. You'll receive an invite email with an activation link.

Roles & what each can see

Role Access
Recruiter Works only with candidates and pipelines tied to the work orders assigned to them. Full workflow access on those reqs: sourcing, outreach, interviews, evaluations.
Recruiter Admin Everything a recruiter has, across the whole team, plus the Approvals queue, Team management (invite, edit roles), and governance/decision queues (view). Can manage line staff in their own tenant.
Tenant Admin Adds subscription/billing management and tenant-level settings.
Hiring Manager A focused view: dashboard, open roles, candidates awaiting their approval or interview feedback. Cannot trigger sourcing or change AI settings.
Platform Admin Lexy staff only: client management, platform candidate pool, system health, fairness dashboards.
Recruiter visibility is assignment-based If you can't find a candidate or work order you expect to see, check whether the req is assigned to you. Ask your Recruiter Admin to assign it — visibility follows assignment, by design.

Finding your way around

  • Sidebar (left, always dark): Dashboard, Work Orders, Approvals (admins), Candidates, Internal Talent, Sourcing, Outreach & Inbox, Engagement, Anti-Ghost, Analytics, Market Intel, LINX Queue, AI Job Queue, Team, Settings.
  • Light / dark theme: toggle in the header. The interview room always stays dark for candidates by design.
  • Scores everywhere: a score shown as — means "not evaluated yet", which is different from a real 0. Lexy never fakes a number.

3. Dashboard & Morning Report

The Dashboard is your daily home base.

Morning Report

  • A daily digest compiled by the Digest Agent: what happened overnight, what needs your attention today, and direct links ("doors") into each item.
  • Every count in the report matches exactly what you'll see when you click through — same filters, same compliance rules. If a count excludes erased or do-not-contact candidates, the report says so.

KPI cards

  • Roles in Motion — active work orders with pipeline activity.
  • Total Candidates — your visible bench (excludes erased/DNC records).
  • AI Interview Sessions — completed and in-flight structured interviews.
  • Hires — placements recorded via the offer funnel.

Clicking a KPI opens the exact list behind the number — the count and the list always agree.

Other dashboard widgets

  • AI Command Center — start a sourcing run or screening task without leaving the dashboard.
  • Pipeline Funnel — candidate distribution across stages, clickable per stage.
  • Hire-Ready Candidates — your highest-scored, furthest-progressed people right now.
  • Agent Activity Feed — a real-time, truthful log of what the agents actually did (with real timestamps). "Agents online" reflects genuinely active runs and live interviews.
  • Live Run view — while a sourcing/screening run is executing, the dashboard can take over with the latest events streaming in; the elapsed clock stops when the run finishes.

4. Work Orders (Jobs)

A Work Order is a hiring requisition: the job, its ideal candidate profile, its pipeline, its interviews and its analytics — all in one workspace.

Creating a work order

  1. Go to Work Orders → New Work Order.
  2. Paste or write the job description. The richer the JD, the better the ICP the AI extracts.
  3. Fill in title, department, location, work type (on-site / hybrid / remote — remote roles skip location filtering in sourcing), and compensation if known.
  4. Optionally record your client's own requisition number — Lexy also assigns its own internal Work Order # automatically; the two are kept separate and both are searchable.
  5. Submit. Depending on your tenant's policy the work order may enter Pending Approval.

The approval gate

Nothing runs on an unapproved job While a work order is in pending approval, draft, or rejected status, Lexy blocks all candidate actions on it: no sourcing, no outreach, no interview invites, no interview links — even previously issued links stop working. Approvers (Recruiter Admins / Hiring Managers) act from the Approvals queue. Once approved, everything unlocks.

Work order tabs

Tab What you do there
Overview Status, department, assigned recruiter, aggregate stats, approval state.
ICP Review and edit the AI-extracted Ideal Candidate Profile (Section 5).
Sourcing Trigger agent runs, review sourced matches, opt in to platform recommendations.
Pipeline The Kanban board (Section 7).
Candidates Flat list of every candidate linked to this job, with a live search box that filters by name, title, company, source or email as you type.
Interviews Sessions for this req: schedule, copy invite links, watch status, open reports.
Anti-Ghost Pipeline health score, stale-candidate and no-show alerts, nurture settings.
Tip The header of each candidate row shows their match score pill. Bands: 80+ (strong), 60–79 (good), below 60 (stretch). A dash means not scored yet.

5. The Ideal Candidate Profile (ICP)

The ICP is the contract between you and the sourcing/screening agents. Time invested here pays back across the entire pipeline.

What the ICP contains

  • Must-have skills and nice-to-have skills (chips you can add/remove).
  • Seniority & years of experience ranges.
  • Certifications required or preferred.
  • Disqualifiers — hard "no" criteria the screener enforces.
  • Location preference — a first-class field. If you clear it, it means "no location preference" (Lexy will not silently fall back to the job's address). Out-of-area candidates are kept but flagged, never hidden.

Editing the ICP

  1. Open Work Order → ICP. Each section renders as editable chips.
  2. Click a chip to remove it; use the add field to insert new criteria.
  3. Changes save against the latest ICP version — edits are immediate and used by the very next sourcing or screening run.
Who can edit Recruiters can edit the ICP of reqs assigned to them. On unassigned reqs, ICP changes are admin-only.
Tip for remote roles Set the job's work type to remote — external sourcing will then search without any location constraint. For located roles, sourcing automatically widens city → region → country if the exact city returns nothing, and labels each result with the tier it came from.

6. Sourcing & AI Agent Runs

Internal first

Lexy always looks at your own bench before spending on external search. External sourcing only unlocks after the internal review step is completed for the req — this is enforced by the platform, not just suggested.

Starting a run

  1. From the Work Order's Sourcing tab (or the dashboard Command Center), click Run Sourcing.
  2. If a run is already in progress for that job, Lexy opens the existing run instead of starting a duplicate.
  3. Watch progress live: each step (ICP check, internal search, external providers, scoring, dedup) streams as events.

What a run produces

  • A ranked list of sourced candidates with match scores, source labels (internal bench, PDL, web search) and a location-tier label for external results.
  • Automatic deduplication — one candidate record per person per tenant, keyed by email. If a sourced person matches an existing record you'll be prompted to merge rather than creating a duplicate.
  • New candidates land in the Sourced lane of the pipeline and are automatically resume-screened when a resume is available.

Platform Recommendations

If you opt a job in (Sourcing tab toggle), Lexy's platform pool can push consenting, privacy-screened candidates into your pipeline. Candidates control their own discoverability; you only ever see people who opted in.

Agent Hub & AI Job Queue

  • Agent Hub shows each agent's recent runs, success rates and lets you trigger "Run now".
  • AI Job Queue lists queued background AI tasks (screenings, rescores, enrichments) and their statuses. Duplicate work is automatically collapsed.
  • Runs interrupted by a platform restart are labeled interrupted, not failed — just re-run them.

7. The Pipeline Board (Kanban)

The Pipeline tab is where you move people through the hiring flow.

Lanes

Working lanes follow the typical flow Sourced → Screened/Verified → Outreach → Interview stages → Offer stages → Hired. Exact lane names on your board may vary slightly by workspace configuration (for example, verification and hiring-manager review may appear as their own lanes). Terminal stages don't clutter the board:

  • Hired candidates appear in a celebratory wins strip.
  • Rejected / withdrawn / closed candidates live in the Closed drawer — open it to review or Restore someone back into a working lane.

Cards

  • Each card shows name, current title/company, match score pill, and status badges (screening verdict, verification flag, interview state).
  • Copy interview link right from the card once a session exists — the link is the exact magic link the candidate uses. If your browser blocks the clipboard, Lexy shows the link in a prompt for manual copy.
  • Action buttons advance the offer funnel (offer extended, accepted, declined) and record outcomes for analytics.

Moving candidates

  • Drag a card between lanes, or use the stage menu on the card.
  • Your manual move always wins. If an agent gate would normally hold a candidate (e.g. verification pending before outreach), an explicit recruiter move overrides it.
  • Moving into Interview can trigger the interview invitation flow; moving into Rejected can trigger a brand-voice rejection email.
  • Need a silent move (data cleanup, recording history)? Use “skip automation” in the move dialog — the candidate changes stage with no emails, invites, or agent runs fired.

Stage automation summary

Entering stage What Lexy does automatically
Sourced / linked to job Resume screening runs (if a resume exists); match score computed.
Verify Verification agent runs for every candidate entering the stage.
Outreach Outreach agent drafts a personalized email → waits in your approval queue.
Interview Interview invitation (magic link) can be generated and sent.
Rejected AI-written, brand-voice rejection email (with a safe template fallback).
Positive replies auto-advance When a sourced candidate replies positively to outreach, Lexy classifies the reply and can advance them to Interview automatically, sending the interview invite in the same motion.

8. Candidates & the 360° Profile

The Candidates list

  • Your full visible bench, grouped into Matched (has accrued intelligence) and Unmatched.
  • Filters for stage, source, activity. "Active" is derived from real recent activity.
  • Erased (GDPR) and do-not-contact candidates are excluded everywhere — lists, counts and analytics alike.

The 360° profile

Area Details
Header Name, title, company, contact info, Fit Score ring (0–100 hire probability), current stage, trust/verification badge.
Intelligence The accrued multi-dimension score (technical, domain, trajectory, engagement, and more). Dimensions not yet evidenced show —. This accrued score is the source of truth on every surface — point-in-time match scores are only a fallback.
Resume Screening Extracted work history, matched vs. missing skills, AI recommendation and reasoning.
Interviews Every session: recording player, per-question grades, transcript, proctoring summary.
Verification Identity/employment consistency results and any flags. The badge appears only when a real verification result exists.
Timeline Chronological event feed: stage changes, emails sent/received, interviews, notes, outcome events.
Connection strength An engagement score that only grows through real completed interactions. A zero is honest — it means no completed engagement yet, not an error.
Trust gate When a candidate's trust score is low or unverified, primary surfaces lead with the gate badge so you see the caveat before the score.

Adding candidates manually

  • Add candidate — form entry; if the email matches an existing record you'll get a merge prompt instead of a duplicate.
  • Bulk import — CSV/spreadsheet upload; supports flagging rows as current employees for the internal-mobility bench.
  • Parse CVs — drop resumes; Lexy extracts profiles and links them to the job (only on approved jobs).

9. Resume Screening & Verification

Resume screening

  • Runs automatically whenever a candidate with a resume is linked to a job — sourced, imported or manually added.
  • Produces: recommendation (advance / review / reject), matched skills, missing skills, summary, and evidence quotes.
  • Skills found in the resume are merged into the candidate's skill set, and the match score is recomputed after enrichment.
  • The screening verdict drives the default Kanban lane — but your manual placement always overrides it, and a human rejection is never overwritten by a re-screen.

Fairness by construction

What the AI never sees Every AI scorer runs behind a fairness firewall: personally identifying details (name, photo, contact info, demographic hints) are redacted before scoring, and every scoring prompt carries a fairness directive. Candidates with sparse profiles are scored on the skills they show — absence of a job title is not treated as a mismatch.

Verification

  • Runs automatically for every candidate entering the Verify stage.
  • Checks profile consistency (employment claims, public footprint) and produces a verdict with flags.
  • While it runs, the profile shows a live "verifying…" indicator; the badge appears only when a real result lands.
  • A pending verification never blocks you: an explicit stage move overrides the gate.

10. Outreach, Approvals & the Inbox

How outreach works

  1. A candidate enters the Outreach stage (or you enroll them in a sequence).
  2. The Outreach Agent writes a personalized email using your tenant's brand voice, documents and the job context.
  3. The draft lands in Pending approvals. Nothing sends without you (unless your admin has explicitly enabled autopilot for a campaign).

Reviewing drafts

  • Open Outreach → Pending. Each draft shows the candidate, job, subject and body.
  • Edit in place — click into subject or body, adjust, and save. Then approve.
  • Approve queues the send; Discard drops the draft.
  • A candidate with no usable email can't be approved — the "no email on file" gate is non-overridable; add an email to the profile first.

Sequences & campaigns

  • Build multi-step sequences with delays and A/B subject variants in the Campaign Manager.
  • Step messages honor the same approval rules; edits are per-message.
  • Every send is deduplicated — a candidate never receives the same first touch twice.

The Inbox

  • Replies flow into a shared, tenant-wide inbox — every reply is captured even if a req has no assigned recruiter.
  • The Conversation Agent classifies each reply: Interested, Not now, Question, Unsubscribe.
  • Interested replies can auto-advance the candidate to Interview and trigger the invite.
  • Unsubscribe adds the candidate to the Do-Not-Contact list immediately; DNC is enforced platform-wide.
  • Where your tenant uses Microsoft 365, replies to recruiter mailboxes sync into Lexy automatically.
Kill switch Admins have a tenant-wide kill switch that halts every AI-generated message instantly — use it if anything ever looks wrong with automated sending.

11. AI Interviews (end to end)

Lexy conducts live, voice-based structured interviews. The interviewer is always "Lexy" — a consistent, calibrated persona.

Interview types

  • Screening interview — role-based structured screen for a specific work order.
  • Technical interview — includes coding/answer submissions where configured.
  • Mock & baseline interviews — candidate-initiated practice from their portal (results visible to the candidate only).

Inviting a candidate

  1. From the pipeline card, candidate profile, or Work Order → Interviews, choose Invite to interview.
  2. Lexy generates a magic link — a private URL that is itself the candidate's credential. No account or password needed.
  3. Send it via the built-in email, or click Copy link on the pipeline card to share it yourself.
  4. When you generate a link yourself, re-clicking reuses the existing session rather than creating a duplicate. (Invites triggered automatically by a positive outreach reply create their own fresh session.)
Approval gate applies Interview links only work on approved work orders. If a job is pushed back to pending/rejected, existing links stop working until it's re-approved.

Directing the interview

  • In the Work Order's interview settings you can set a focus directive ("probe system design depth") and add custom questions for all interview types.
  • Custom questions are guaranteed to be asked verbatim; they replace some of the generated questions so total length stays the same.
  • Defaults can be set once per job and apply to every session created after.

What the candidate experiences

  1. Opens the magic link → sees the start screen with the job context and, if configured, a personal video message from you (Section 12).
  2. Gives explicit consent to AI interviewing and recording — the interview cannot start without it, and revoking mid-interview halts all capture instantly.
  3. Grants mic/camera access. Lexy speaks each question aloud; the candidate answers naturally by voice. Works on desktop and mobile, in multiple languages (the session language follows the invite).
  4. Long answers are safe: Lexy waits while the candidate is actively speaking and never cuts off mid-thought.
  5. Lexy closes the interview gracefully when the conversation reaches a natural farewell.

After the interview

  • Per-question grades — each answer is scored once at completion with reasoning; ungraded items show —, and a genuine 0 shows as 0.
  • Summary & overall score, competency breakdown (calibrated so competency averages can't inflate above the graded overall).
  • Recording — full audio/video playback in the candidate profile.
  • Proctoring signals — face presence, gaze, tab switches; the risk level, flags and notes always agree with the same per-signal thresholds shown in the UI.
  • Work authorization — asked as a separate non-scored step, stored as compliance data, never fed to the scorer.
  • On completion the candidate is automatically advanced past the Interview stage in your pipeline.
  • No-shows — an overdue scheduled interview flips to No show automatically and notifies the req owner; Anti-Ghost can nurture from there.

12. Recruiter Intro Videos

Put a human face on the process: candidates see "A message from your recruiter" before their interview starts.

  1. Go to Settings → Intro Video (or your profile settings).
  2. Upload a photo of yourself and pick a voice/tone. Lexy generates a short, personalized avatar video (per language, cached and reused).
  3. Preview the script first, tweak the tone, then generate.
  4. Every interview invite for your reqs now shows your video on the candidate's start screen. If generation is unavailable, candidates see a branded Lexy fallback — the interview is never blocked.

13. Evaluation Reports & Hiring-Manager Sharing

The Candidate Evaluation Report

  • A client-facing report for a specific candidate × role: profile summary, match analysis, interview assessment, verification status, recommendation.
  • Generated automatically the first time you export; it carries a draft notice until you approve it. Approve once you've reviewed — subsequent exports drop the notice.
  • Export as PDF from the candidate page, the interview page, or attach it when sharing with a hiring manager — these surfaces use the same evaluation report (a simpler legacy export exists as a fallback in rare cases).

Sharing with a Hiring Manager (no login needed)

  1. From the candidate profile choose Share with hiring manager.
  2. Lexy creates a private, expiring link containing a sanitized snapshot: evaluation, resume, interview highlights. No login required — the link itself is the credential; treat it like a confidential document.
  3. The HM can record a decision (advance / reject / discuss) and leave feedback directly in the page.
  4. Their decision and comments land in your inbox and on the candidate's timeline.
Attachments open correctly Resumes attach in their true format (PDF or Word) — Lexy detects the file type so hiring managers never receive an unopenable file.

14. Talent Match, Rediscovery & Internal Talent

Talent Match

  • On a candidate: see every role they fit across your visible reqs, with per-role fit scores.
  • On a job: see the best matches from your existing bench before sourcing externally.
  • Scores are computed against the job's context; where no stored score exists yet, a deterministic signal-based estimate is shown (never a random number).

Talent Rediscovery

  • Surfaces past candidates (silver medalists, previous applicants) who now match a new req.
  • Reading/rediscovering is open across your agency; acting (linking to a req, outreach) requires the req to be assigned to you.

Internal Talent

  • A dedicated internal-mobility search over your own employees and bench (marked via import or the employee toggle on a profile).
  • Strictly firewalled to your tenant pool — internal search never mixes in external or platform candidates.
  • This is also the "internal first" step that unlocks external sourcing spend (Section 6).

Staleness — fair but honest

Long-inactive candidates gently rank lower in match lists (never hidden, scores never rewritten). Lexy also runs a 6-month check-in program that emails quiet candidates asking them to refresh their status; self-reported job changes re-engage them automatically.

15. Engagement & Anti-Ghost

Engagement

  • Tracks candidate interest signals: email opens/replies, portal activity, interview completion.
  • Feeds the connection-strength score on each profile (only real completed events count).

Anti-Ghost

  • Pipeline Health per work order: a score with the specific candidates dragging it down.
  • Alerts for: no replies after N touches, interview no-shows, stale in-stage candidates.
  • Nurture actions: schedule a follow-up touch (goes through the same approval flow), or hand back to you with a suggested next step.
  • Candidate status check-ins keep long-term bench data fresh automatically.

16. Analytics, Calibration & Fairness

Hiring analytics

  • Funnel — stage-to-stage conversion for your reqs, split by how candidates entered (sourced vs. applied).
  • Source quality — hire-rate and progression by source.
  • Outcomes — offers extended/accepted/declined, time-to-hire.
  • All analytics respect the same compliance filters as your lists (erased/DNC candidates are excluded and labeled as such).

AI calibration

  • Override rate — how often your team disagrees with AI recommendations, per agent.
  • Learning insights — where the AI's scoring is drifting from your actual decisions, with suggested adjustments.
  • Per-tenant learned scoring only ever activates after enough evidence and after beating the baseline in a backtest — otherwise Lexy stays on defaults.

Fairness monitoring

  • Adverse-impact monitoring (4/5ths rule) over stage progression, computed on anonymized aggregates.
  • Demographics are firewalled from all scoring; they exist only in aggregate fairness views.
  • Sourcing diversity views are k-anonymized — no individual is ever identifiable.

17. Market Intelligence & Ask Lexy

  • Hiring Velocity — how fast similar roles are moving on the platform.
  • Candidate Supply — depth of available talent for a skill/region.
  • Comp Signal — salary expectation ranges, built only from cross-tenant aggregates (never individual records).
  • Internal Bench — your own availability before you buy externally.
  • Ask Lexy — a natural-language chat over these tools. Every answer cites its data source, and when there isn't enough data Lexy says "no data" rather than guessing.

18. LINX Queue (agency ↔ client collaboration)

  • LINX lets a client tenant send staffing requests to your agency (and vice versa) without sharing full workspaces.
  • Incoming requests appear in the LINX Queue with the role details and requesting party.
  • Accept clones the request into a work order in your own tenant; Decline returns it with a reason.
  • Both sides can see the request status; only the originating side can withdraw it.

19. Team, Settings & Subscription

Team (admins)

  • Invite teammates by email with a role; they activate via the invite link.
  • Edit roles — Recruiter Admins can manage line staff in their own tenant; only higher roles can promote to admin.
  • Assign work orders to recruiters — remember, assignment controls what a recruiter can see.
  • The member count reflects staff only (candidates with portal accounts are never counted as team members).

Settings

  • Brand & voice — company description, tone, documents. This context flavors every candidate-facing email Lexy writes, including rejections.
  • Intro video — your personal avatar video (Section 12).
  • Interview defaults — per-job focus directives and custom questions.
  • Notifications — email digests and reply alerts.
  • Theme — light/dark.

Subscription (tenant admins)

  • Country-based subscription with pricing per country; status moves automatically through active → grace → suspended based on paid-through date.
  • Recording a payment is the only manual step; everything else is automatic.

20. Compliance, Privacy & Candidate Consent

  • Interview consent — candidates must explicitly consent before any AI interview capture; revocation stops recording, transcription and scoring immediately, mid-interview.
  • Do Not Contact — one unsubscribe anywhere blocks all future automated contact platform-wide.
  • Erasure (GDPR) — erased candidates vanish from every list, count, export and analytic.
  • Candidate privacy controls — platform-pool candidates control their discoverability; pausing hides them everywhere including from historical "viewed you" style surfaces.
  • Candidate-facing framing — candidates never see raw low scores; their portal shows percentile bands and growth guidance. What you see internally is more detailed than what they see.
  • EEO & work authorization — demographic questions are never asked in interviews; work-authorization capture is separate from scoring.
  • EU AI Act — Lexy maintains decision audit logs, deviation panels and documentation surfaces for regulated-market clients.
  • Data isolation — every tenant's data is isolated; agencies see their client sub-tenants only through explicit hierarchy, and recruiters see only assigned reqs.
Your responsibilities Treat magic links and HM share links as confidential. Don't paste candidate PII into outside tools. Record real outcomes (offers, hires, rejections) — calibration and fairness monitoring depend on truthful data.

21. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Symptom Likely cause & fix
"I can't see a candidate/work order I know exists." The req isn't assigned to you. Ask your Recruiter Admin to assign it.
"The interview link says the interview isn't available." The work order isn't approved (or was pushed back). Approve it and the link works again.
"Sourcing found nobody." Location too narrow (Lexy auto-widens, but check the ICP location), or internal review not yet completed for external spend.
"Outreach draft won't approve." Candidate has no email on file — add one to the profile first. This gate cannot be overridden.
"A score shows a dash." Not evaluated yet — that's honest, not broken. Trigger the relevant step (screening, interview) to get a score.
"Work Order Not Found on a job I just had open." You're logged in as a different user in another tab. Log back in as yourself; visibility is per-user.
"Copy link didn't copy." Your browser blocked clipboard access; Lexy shows the link in a dialog — copy it manually.
"Candidate says the interview froze." Rare connectivity issue; the session is resumable — have them reopen the same link.
"Duplicate candidate warning when adding." A record with that email already exists. Choose merge — Lexy never creates duplicates by email.
