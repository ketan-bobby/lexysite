Every interview builds a verified, dynamic career profile based on actual performance. Those shared signals power faster, fairer, and more accurate matches between talent and teams.

What we deliver:
• 10 specialized AI agents working across sourcing, screening, interviewing, and verification
• Multilingual interviews, so great candidates aren't filtered out by language
• 3× faster time-to-hire for employers
• 94% candidate satisfaction — because candidates get a fair chance to show what they can do

Every AI recommendation on Lexy is reviewed by a human recruiter before it reaches a client. AI does the heavy lifting; people make the decisions.
