1. What is Lexy?

The big picture in plain English

Lexy is an AI-powered hiring platform. Think of it as a highly intelligent assistant that sits between companies looking to hire and people looking for work — and does most of the heavy lifting that normally takes recruiters weeks to do by hand.


Instead of a recruiter spending their day writing job descriptions, searching LinkedIn, emailing candidates one by one, chasing people who go quiet, and trying to guess which candidates are most likely to succeed — Lexy automates all of that and makes smart recommendations at every step.


At the same time, candidates get their own private space where Lexy learns about them through a friendly conversation, matches them to real opportunities, and tells them exactly what they need to do to land their dream job.

💡

The analogy that makes it click

Imagine hiring a team of 10 expert assistants, each one a specialist — one who reads CVs, one who conducts interviews, one who sends emails, one who monitors fraud, one who never lets a good candidate slip through the cracks. Now make all 10 of them work 24/7 and never forget a detail. That's Lexy.

👥

2. Who uses Lexy?

Three different types of users, one connected system

🏢

Platform Admin

The people who run Lexy itself. They can see everything across all companies, manage who gets access, and control the overall platform. Think of them as the landlord of the whole system.

🏬

Tenant (Agency or Company)

A recruitment agency or a company that uses Lexy to hire people. They might have multiple branches or offices (called subsidiaries). Each one has its own recruiters, its own jobs, and its own candidates. They only see their own data.

🤵

Recruiter

The humans inside a company or agency who do the actual hiring work. They use Lexy to find candidates, run the hiring process, and make final decisions. Lexy does all the grunt work; they make the judgment calls.

🙋

Candidate

Job seekers who create a profile on the platform. They answer questions in a conversational interview, get matched to jobs automatically, and receive personalised guidance on their career — all without filling in a single form.

💼

3. The Recruiter Side

What recruiters see and can do inside the platform

When a recruiter logs in, they get a full command centre. Here's what every section does:

📋

Job Board

Create and manage job openings. Each job has a full description, salary range, required skills, and location. Lexy uses this information to automatically find and match the right people.

🎯

Talent Match

Lexy scours its database (including people who registered themselves) and presents a ranked list of who best matches each job. The list shows a match score and explains why each person is a good fit.

📬

Outreach Campaigns

Instead of a recruiter writing individual emails, Lexy generates personalised messages for each candidate and sends them automatically. It then tracks who opened the email, who replied, and who needs a follow-up.

📥

Recruiter Inbox

All replies from candidates land here — neatly organised by which job they're about, not one big jumbled list. Lexy reads each reply and labels it: "Positive", "Question", or "Needs Follow-up" so the recruiter knows exactly what to do next.

🧠

AI Agents Panel

A live dashboard showing all 10 AI agents and what they're currently doing. Recruiters can see agent activity, start or stop specific agents, and review their recommendations.

⚖️

Decision Queue

When the AI thinks a candidate is ready to be advanced (or rejected), it puts that suggestion in a queue for the recruiter to review. The recruiter either agrees or overrides it — and the AI learns from the decision.

🏢

Client Management

For agencies managing multiple client companies, this section lets them control access, grant permissions, and manage which company can see which candidates. Each company is kept completely separate.

🚫

Do Not Contact (DNC) List

If someone asks not to be contacted (or unsubscribes), they're added to a protected list. Lexy will never send that person a message again — even automatically. This keeps the company legally compliant and respectful.

4. Finding the Right Candidates

How Lexy sources, scores, and ranks people

🗄️

Three Pools of Candidates

Lexy draws from three sources of people. First, the company's own private database — people they've already worked with or who applied directly. Second, the platform-wide pool — candidates who registered on Lexy themselves and agreed to be found by any qualifying company. Third, people sourced from external sources like LinkedIn or job boards.

🔄 How matching works, step by step

1

The recruiter posts a job

They describe the role, skills needed, experience level, salary range, and any specific requirements. This creates what Lexy calls an "Ideal Candidate Profile" (ICP).

2

Lexy searches its database

The Sourcing Agent scans all available candidates — comparing their skills, experience, location, and career history against the ICP. It scores each person on how well they match.

3

A ranked shortlist appears

Candidates are ranked from strongest match to weakest. Each card shows the person's name, current job title, match score, key strengths, and any gaps or concerns.

4

The recruiter approves the outreach

The recruiter selects who to contact. Lexy then writes a personalised message for each person and — with approval — sends it automatically on the recruiter's behalf.

5

Replies are classified automatically

When candidates write back, Lexy reads the reply and categorises it — interested, asking questions, not interested, out of office — and routes it to the recruiter's inbox with a suggested next action.

⚡

Anti-Ghosting Protection

One of the biggest problems in hiring is candidates who go silent. Lexy tracks every candidate's engagement and, if someone hasn't responded, automatically sends gentle follow-up messages at the right time — so good candidates don't slip away because of a missed email.

5. The Hiring Pipeline

How a candidate moves from "found" to "hired"

Every candidate goes through a series of stages. Lexy tracks exactly where each person is and tells the recruiter what to do next.

🎉

Hired

📄

Screening

Lexy reads the candidate's CV and profile, checks their skills against the job requirements, and produces a match score with a written summary. The recruiter doesn't have to read every CV manually.

🎤

AI Interviews

Lexy conducts a structured voice or text interview with the candidate. It scores them on communication, technical depth, and culture fit — then writes a summary report so the recruiter gets a full picture without sitting in on every call.

🛡️

Verification

Before making an offer, Lexy checks that the candidate's claimed work history, identity, and qualifications appear legitimate. It flags anything that looks inconsistent so the recruiter can investigate.

👁️

Interview Proctoring

During AI interviews, Lexy monitors for unusual behaviour — like someone else answering on the candidate's behalf. It logs a "trust score" so the recruiter knows how authentic the interview was.

6. The 10 AI Agents

The specialists working behind the scenes, 24/7

Think of these as 10 permanent employees, each with a specific expertise. They work automatically in the background and hand off their findings to the recruiter.

3 📋 Screening Agent Reviews CVs and profiles in detail. Highlights strengths, flags gaps, and gives each candidate a fit score out of 100. Automatic
4 🎤 Interview Agent Conducts structured interviews with candidates using voice or text. Scores their communication, technical depth, and behaviour. Produces a written report. With candidate

8 👻 Anti-Ghosting Agent Detects when a candidate has gone quiet. Automatically sends re-engagement messages at the right time to bring them back into the conversation before they disappear. Automatic
9 📅 Scheduling Agent Handles interview scheduling. Detects if someone is likely to not show up and sends reminders or alternatives to reduce no-shows. Automatic
10 📊 Analytics Agent Tracks how the overall hiring process is performing — how many people reach each stage, what the average hire time is, where candidates drop off — and uses that to improve future recommendations. Reporting

7. The Predictive Hiring Engine

The brain that scores, predicts, and recommends

The Predictive Hiring Engine is the most powerful part of Lexy. It continuously analyses every signal it has on a candidate — their CV match, interview performance, identity verification, engagement level, and behavioural signals — and produces a single, clear recommendation: what should happen next, and why.

🎯

Fit Score

How well does the candidate's background, skills, and experience align with what the role actually requires? A high fit score means this is the right person for the right job.

⭐

Quality Score

How good is the candidate, independent of the role? This covers communication skills, technical depth, interview performance, and how they compare to the broader candidate pool.

🛡️

Trust Score

Can we trust what the candidate has told us? This blends identity verification, CV consistency checks, and interview integrity signals into a single number.

⚡

Conversion Score

How likely is this person to actually complete the process without going quiet or dropping out? This looks at engagement history, responsiveness, and ghosting risk.

🔍

What the 4 questions tell you

Hire likelihood tells you the overall probability this person gets hired. Predicted risk tells you the exact point in the process where they're most likely to stumble. Confidence tells you how much data Lexy has — the more agents have assessed this person, the more confident the prediction. Why breaks down the specific signals driving the score up or down in plain English.

📚

The engine learns over time

Every time a recruiter overrides Lexy's recommendation — and especially when the recruiter turns out to be right — the engine records it. Over time, Lexy gets better at predicting which candidates succeed at your specific company, with your specific hiring managers, for your specific types of roles.

8. The Candidate Portal

What job seekers experience when they use Lexy

Candidates don't fill in forms. They have a conversation. When someone registers on Lexy, they go through a friendly 10-minute AI interview that learns everything about them — their experience, skills, communication style, career goals, and what kind of opportunity they're looking for. From that single conversation, Lexy builds their entire profile automatically.

🎤 The Candidate Journey

1

Sign up and do the baseline interview

A 10-minute conversational interview. Lexy asks about work history, skills, what the candidate is looking for, and their career ambitions. No forms. No CV upload required. Just a natural back-and-forth.

2

Get a career profile and visibility score

After the interview, Lexy creates a complete profile. A "Visibility Score" (0–100%) shows how discoverable the candidate is — the more they complete, the more likely recruiters are to find them.

3

See matched job opportunities

The Opportunity Engine shows jobs that genuinely match the candidate's profile. Jobs currently hiring are shown in green. Jobs that are paused but coming soon are shown in amber — the candidate can register interest and get notified when they open.

4

Practise interviews

Candidates can do as many practice interviews as they like. Lexy gives them a mock interview for any role and scores their performance — helping them prepare and get better before the real thing.

5

Apply with a single click

When a candidate finds a role they like, they can express interest in one click. This sends their full Lexy profile to the recruiter automatically — no separate application form needed.

📊

Visibility Score

A score from 0 to 100% that tells candidates how "findable" they are. Adding skills, completing the interview, and setting career goals all push the score up. Higher scores = more recruiter eyeballs.

🌍

Platform Candidate Pool

Candidates who register on Lexy themselves go into a shared pool that any qualifying company can access. This means a candidate might be discovered by a company they'd never thought to apply to.

🔔

Job Alerts (Coming Soon)

If a candidate sees a job that isn't open yet — a role marked "Coming Soon" — they can register their interest. When the role opens, they get notified first and already have a head start.

📈

Interview Practice

Lexy can run a mock interview for any job the candidate is interested in. It plays the role of the interviewer, asks realistic questions, and gives a score with feedback at the end.

🚀

9. Career Trajectory

Where Lexy helps candidates see their future, not just today

This is where Lexy goes beyond job matching and becomes a genuine career coach. Based on the candidate's interview, skills, and stated goals, Lexy generates up to three personalised career paths — different routes from where they are today to where they want to be in 3–5 years.

🎯

Achievable Path

A realistic route based on the candidate's current skills — the most direct way to reach their goal. Likely to be achievable within 2–3 years with focused effort.

📈

Ambitious Path

A slightly more challenging route that requires developing new skills or making a bigger career leap. High growth potential but will take more deliberate effort.

⚡

Stretch Path

The bold version — a significantly more senior or different role that requires a substantial skills upgrade. Motivating and aspirational, with clear steps on how to get there.

🔮

Future Role Targets in the Opportunity Engine

Inside the Opportunity Engine — alongside today's open roles — Lexy now shows a "Your Career Trajectory" section. This displays each of the candidate's target roles with:

• A readiness percentage — how close they are today to qualifying for that role
• A motivational message — "At your current rate, you could qualify in 3 years"
• The skills they need to build to close the gap
• The target company type and salary range for that role
• A snippet from their own stated 3-year or 5-year goal, making it personal

This section updates automatically as the candidate adds skills, completes interviews, and progresses — so it always reflects where they are right now.

10. How It All Connects

The full picture from first contact to hire

🌐 End-to-end: from a candidate's first visit to their first day on the job

1

Candidate registers and completes their baseline interview

Lexy builds their full profile. They're added to the platform candidate pool and immediately start seeing matched opportunities.

2

A recruiter posts a new job

The Sourcing Agent immediately scans all candidates — including those from the platform pool — and surfaces the best matches, ranked by predicted success.

3

The Outreach Agent sends a personalised message

The candidate receives an email that references their specific background and explains why this role is relevant to their goals. It doesn't feel like a mass mail-out.

4

The candidate replies (or Lexy follows up automatically)

If the candidate is interested, their reply lands in the recruiter's inbox under the correct job group, labelled "Positive Reply". If they go quiet, the Anti-Ghosting Agent sends a gentle nudge.

5

The Interview Agent conducts a structured assessment

The candidate does an AI interview. The Proctoring Agent ensures the interview is authentic. The results feed directly into the candidate's intelligence record.

6

The Predictive Hiring Engine issues a Lexy Prediction

With all signals collected, Lexy publishes its prediction: hire likelihood, the specific stage where failure is most likely, confidence level, and a plain-English explanation of what's driving the score.

7

The recruiter reviews and decides

The recruiter reads the Lexy Prediction and either agrees with the recommendation or overrides it. If they override it, Lexy records the reasoning and learns from the outcome.

8

Verification and offer

Before the offer goes out, the Verification Agent checks the candidate's identity and history. If everything checks out, the recruiter makes the offer. If the candidate declines, Lexy tracks that too — and uses it to calibrate future predictions.

9

The engine records the outcome and gets smarter

Whether the candidate is hired, rejected, ghosts, or declines — every outcome is fed back into the engine. Over time, Lexy's predictions become more accurate for every company using the platform.
