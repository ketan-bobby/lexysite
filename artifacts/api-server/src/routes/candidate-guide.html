Platform Overview

What is L3xy AI?

L3xy AI is a hiring platform that uses artificial intelligence to help job seekers understand themselves better, prepare for interviews, and get found by the right employers — all through natural conversation rather than forms and checklists.

Instead of submitting a CV and waiting in silence, candidates on L3xy have an AI career partner that maps their background, identifies their strengths and skill gaps, suggests career paths that actually fit them, and gets them ready to interview with confidence.

🎙️

Conversation, not paperwork

L3xy builds a complete picture of who you are through natural dialogue — not forms. The AI listens, adapts to what you share, and draws out the depth of your experience without a single checkbox.

🗺️

Intelligence-led career guidance

Rather than generic suggestions, L3xy maps your background and aspirations to opportunities with genuine alignment — surfacing directions grounded in what you have actually done and where you genuinely want to go.

🏋️

Preparation that builds real confidence

The AI practice engine simulates the rigour of actual interviews, coaches you on both the substance and delivery of your answers, and gives you a measurable view of how your readiness is improving session by session.

🔍

Visibility that works for you

A fully built profile puts you in front of the right recruiters without the effort of active searching. The stronger your profile, the more relevant opportunities come to you — passively and continuously.

🌏

Interview in your strongest language

L3xy supports interviews across multiple languages, so candidates can articulate their experience and ambitions with the nuance, precision, and confidence that only comes from speaking in the language they think in.

📊

Every number earned, not estimated

Your readiness score, profile strength, and career analysis are derived exclusively from your real data. No manufactured benchmarks, no fabricated averages. What you see reflects where you actually stand.

Candidate Journey Storyboard

The Candidate Experience, Step by Step

Here is exactly what a candidate sees and does from the moment they join L3xy AI to the point where they are interview-ready and visible to employers.

🚀 Step 1
Arrival

Sign Up & First Impressions

A candidate arrives on the L3xy candidate portal — either by clicking a link sent by a recruiter, or by registering directly. The interface is dark, modern, and immediately communicates that this is not a typical job board.

If they were invited by a recruiter for a specific role, their onboarding is pre-linked to that opportunity. Otherwise, they join the open talent pool and start with a general career assessment.

What they see

A welcome screen with their name, a brief explanation of what comes next, and a single clear call to action: start the career interview.

What happens behind the scenes

L3xy creates their candidate profile in the database and, if they came from a recruiter link, tags their account to that specific role and company.

Sign-up Recruiter invite or self-register Multi-language ready from day 1
📄 Step 2
Profile Setup

Upload a CV or Build from Scratch

Before the AI interview, L3xy gives candidates the option to upload their existing CV. If they do, the AI reads it automatically and pre-fills their work history, skills, education, and current role — so they don't have to type it all out again.

If they don't have a CV ready, they can skip this and let the AI interview build their profile from the conversation alone. Nothing is blocked; they can always add a CV later.

CV upload

Drag and drop a PDF or Word document. The AI extracts your job history, skills, qualifications, and experience automatically.

No CV? No problem

Skip it and start the interview. The AI builds your profile from the conversation. You can upload a CV at any time from your Settings page.

PDF & Word supported AI reads your CV automatically Fully optional at this stage
🎙️ Step 3
Core Experience

The Baseline Career Interview

This is the heart of L3xy. An AI called Lexy conducts a friendly, spoken conversation — approximately 10 minutes — asking about the candidate's background, what they enjoy doing, what they're looking for next, where they want to be in their career, and what matters most to them.

It does not feel like a test. Lexy listens, follows up naturally, and adapts its questions based on what the candidate shares. The candidate can speak in English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, or several other Indian languages — Lexy responds in the same language.

After the interview is complete, L3xy's AI analyses the full conversation and produces a detailed written profile: a summary of the candidate's background, their identified strengths, areas for growth, communication style, and career readiness.

What the AI asks about

Current role and experience · Career goals (1, 3, 5 years) · What kinds of work energise them · Where they feel less confident · What they want from a new role

What the AI produces

A written profile summary · Identified strength areas · Growth areas (skill gaps) · Communication style assessment · An initial readiness score

~10 minutes Spoken or typed — candidate's choice 8 Indian languages supported Full transcript saved
🗺️ Step 4
Career Intelligence

Your Career Hub — Everything in One Place

After the baseline interview, the candidate enters their Career Hub — a personal dashboard that becomes their home on L3xy. This is where they see everything about where they stand and what to do next.

Readiness Score

A 0–100 score showing how interview-ready you are. It updates every time you practise, improve your profile, or do another interview session. The score is computed from your actual activity — not an estimate.

Career Paths

3–5 suggested career directions based on your background and goals. Each path shows what the role looks like, what skills you already have, and what you'd need to develop to get there.

Skill Gaps

Specific skills identified from your interview where you have room to grow. These are high-impact skills for the roles you're targeting — not generic recommendations.

Strengths

The things you are already good at, identified from your answers. These are shown so you can lead with them in interviews and applications.

Next Best Actions

A prioritised list of the 2–3 most valuable things you can do today to move your career forward. Always computed from your actual gaps — never generic advice.

Progress Tracking

A week-by-week view of how your readiness and profile strength are improving. You can see exactly how much progress you've made since your last session.

All data from your profile & interview Updates in real time No fake averages or estimates
🏋️ Step 5
Preparation

AI Practice Sessions

Once the candidate knows their strengths and gaps, they can enter the Practice Engine — an AI-powered interview simulator. They pick a mode (quick warm-up, full interview, behavioural, technical, or a mock interview for a specific job) and start practising.

The AI asks realistic interview questions relevant to their target role and level. After each session, they can review coaching tips that help them understand how to answer questions more effectively. Over time, their readiness score reflects the improvement.

Practice modes

Quick warm-up (5 questions) · Full practice interview (10–15 questions) · Behavioural focus · Technical focus · Mock interview for a specific role

After each session

See your transcript · Read general coaching guidance on interview technique · Track your streak (how many days in a row you've practised) · Watch your readiness score climb

5 interview modes Coaching tips after every session Daily streak tracking
🔍 Step 6
Discovery

Matched Opportunities

As the candidate builds their profile, L3xy automatically matches them against live job openings from companies using the platform. The candidate doesn't have to search — relevant roles surface in their Career Hub based on how well they match the job requirements.

Each job shows their seniority level, department, location, work type, and salary range where available. High-relevance matches are labelled "Strong match". They click through to see the full role and apply directly.

How matching works

L3xy compares the candidate's skills, experience, and career goals against the job requirements. Only roles that are genuinely relevant appear — not everything on the platform.

Urgency signals

Some roles are flagged as "Closing soon", "New this week", or "High demand" so candidates know which opportunities to prioritise.

Live job matching No cold searching required Relevance-ranked results
📈 Step 7
Ongoing Growth

Continuous Development & Visibility

L3xy is not a one-time application tool. Candidates who return regularly build a stronger profile over time. Each practice session, each profile update, and each completed interview raises their readiness score and their visibility to recruiters.

The platform tracks streaks (consecutive days of activity), flags when someone has been inactive for more than five days, and always surfaces the single most important action to take next — so candidates always know where to focus their energy.

Recruiter Visibility Score

A score (0–100) showing how discoverable your profile is to recruiters. It rises as you complete your interview, add skills, set career goals, and strengthen your profile. The score is honest — it reflects your actual profile signals, not invented activity data.

Career Analysis (unlockable)

After completing practice sessions, candidates can unlock a deeper view of their interview performance — including communication style feedback, areas for growth, and a detailed breakdown from their AI career analysis.

Streak & activity tracking Profile visibility to recruiters Unlockable deeper analysis

The Technology

The AI Behind the Experience

L3xy AI is powered by a set of specialised AI agents, each responsible for a specific part of the candidate experience. Here is what each one does in plain language.

🎙️

Lexy — The Career Interviewer

Conducts the baseline career interview and all practice sessions. Adapts its questions based on what you say, follows up naturally, and converses in the language you choose. It is the conversational front-end for everything on the platform.

🧠

Career Analyser

After each interview, this agent reads the full transcript and produces a structured analysis: strengths, growth areas, communication style, readiness assessment, and career path suggestions. This analysis is what powers your Career Hub.

📄

CV Reader

When you upload a CV, this agent reads it and extracts your work history, skills, education, and qualifications. It uses this to pre-populate your profile so you don't have to re-enter information you already have on paper.

🗺️

Career Path Engine

Generates personalised career path suggestions based on your interview and profile. For each path, it explains the role, your existing fit, the gaps to close, and what the trajectory looks like. These are created specifically for you — not taken from a generic library.

Data Integrity

What is Real and What is Not

L3xy AI is built on a strict principle: nothing shown to a candidate should be invented. Here is a transparent breakdown of what data is genuinely computed versus what is clearly framed as guidance or estimation.

✅ Fully Real — Computed from Your Data

  • ✓ Readiness score — your actual profile and activity
  • ✓ Profile completeness — real fields counted
  • ✓ Career paths — generated from your interview
  • ✓ Skill gaps & strengths — from your AI analysis
  • ✓ Interview transcript — word-for-word
  • ✓ Career analysis sections — from your own answers
  • ✓ Practice session count — real activity
  • ✓ Day streak — real consecutive days
  • ✓ Matched opportunities — live jobs from DB
  • ✓ Progress delta — compared to last saved snapshot
  • ✓ Benchmark data — real aggregates (when 50+ candidates available)

⚠️ Clearly Framed — Guidance, Not Precision

  • ~ Visibility score — an estimate from 4 profile signals
  • ~ Interview coaching tips — general best-practice guidance, not personalised answer scoring
  • ~ Skill gap label — "high-impact for your path" (not a cross-candidate comparison)
  • ~ Readiness fallback — heuristic estimate until your first snapshot is saved
  • ~ Benchmark comparison — only shown when sample size is large enough (50+ candidates)

These items are clearly labelled in the UI as estimates, guidance, or general coaching. Nothing is presented as precise fact unless it comes from your real data.

Privacy & Trust

How Candidate Data is Handled

Candidates share a lot with L3xy — their career history, their goals, their insecurities, and their ambitions. Here is how that data is protected.

🔒 Our Commitments to Candidates

L3xy AI operates as a multi-tenant platform — each employer organisation is completely isolated. A candidate's data is never shared between employer tenants without their knowledge.

🏢
Tenant Isolation

Each recruiting company sees only candidates who applied to their roles. Your profile is not shared across companies without your consent.

📊
Anonymised Benchmarks

When benchmark data is shown (comparing your readiness to others), it uses anonymised averages. No individual candidate's data is exposed.

🔑
Your Data, Your Control

Candidates can update or delete their profile from the Settings page at any time. CV uploads are stored securely and can be replaced or removed.
